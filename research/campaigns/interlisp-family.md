# Interlisp and Interlisp-D

The October 1978 Interlisp Reference Manual defines the exact language context for the language identity and its scoped technical claims. The revised July 1981 Xerox PARC report documents Interlisp-D on Dolphin and Dorado as a separate implementation identity. The catalog’s shorter report date and the revision printed on the copy are preserved. Later Interlisp-D releases, Interlisp-VAX, and Medley retain separate scopes. [Interlisp Reference Manual](https://softwarepreservation.computerhistory.org/LISP/interlisp/Interlisp-Oct_1978.pdf) · [Papers on Interlisp-D](https://softwarepreservation.computerhistory.org/LISP/interlisp-d/Papers_On_Interlisp-D.pdf)

The broad BBN-LISP catalog heading covers several system contexts. The July 1971 BBN-LISP manual documents the TENEX revision, and the 1978 Interlisp preface describes the subsequent rename of the joint-development system. The original aggregate lead remains unresolved across PDP-1, SDS 940, and PDP-10/TENEX contexts.
