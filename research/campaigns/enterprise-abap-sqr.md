# SQR and ABAP source boundaries

The investigation inspected five existing discovery leads. Separate Codex executions directly acquired the publisher material and checked exact passages and applicable editions. [Independent lead review](../../sces/artifacts/enterprise-locator-review.json) · [Binding witnesses](../../sces/artifacts/enterprise-binding-witnesses.json)

- The October 2022 Oracle PeopleTools 8.60 manual identifies the SQR for PeopleSoft programming language and distinguishes it from separately named tools. The edition on the cover controls the scope despite the PDF filename. [Oracle language reference](https://docs.oracle.com/cd/F70250_01/psft/pdf/pt860tsql-b092022.pdf)
- The original SAP portal lead resolves to the Standard ABAP introduction in the documented `classic_2025` publication, build 10. A separate pinned classifier lead links to the existing ABAP language identity. Its file category and sample leave release compatibility outside scope. [SAP Standard ABAP topic](https://help.sap.com/docs/abap-cloud/abap-keywords-standard-abap/abenabap.html)
- The SAP release 7.54 glossary identifies ABAP CDS DCL. Its language scope is distinct from CDS DDL. The two broader ABAP CDS leads remain unresolved because the classifier and development guide leave the exact language association ambiguous. [SAP ABAP CDS development guide](https://help.sap.com/doc/6bbeffd4801447c0a67b30968778a24f/Dev/en-US/abap_cds_dev_user_guide_EN.pdf)

The original SQR local procedure claim was rejected during direct review. The manual permits an underscore form for global declarations inside local procedures. The independently reviewed revision limits the claim to the documented variable spelling and applicable declaration rules. The rejected revision and its assessment remain in the evidence history. Claims about program entry, variable retention, and ABAP behavior retain their own edition conditions. [Corrected binding review](../../sces/artifacts/sqr-binding-r2-proof-review.jsonl)
