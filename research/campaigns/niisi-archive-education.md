# KuMir and PiktoMir in the NIISI issue archives

Both research executions recovered the 2015 and 2019 issues from the institute's alternate archive and verified their exact contents. The original journal URLs failed certificate validation. Byte equivalence between routes remains unverified. [2015 issue](https://www.niisi.ru/tr/2015_T5_N1.pdf) · [2019 issue](https://www.niisi.ru/tr/2019_T9_N6.pdf)

The 2015 issue uses KuMir as both a language name and a programming system name. The original whole-issue lead remains unresolved. The 2019 issue identifies PiktoMir as an educational environment and names Pikto as a language. The PiktoMir environment lead falls outside the language registry's scope.
