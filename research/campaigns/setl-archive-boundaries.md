# SETL family archive boundaries

Six exact original Computer History Museum archive locators were inspected. They cover LITTLE-based SETL, SETL4, SETL2, ISETL, ISETL-LINDA, and SetlX. The archived inventory matched SHA-256 `70f055f3ca256d804170e2f58a055ef277950c1855f9d3d1d9659950ced52a05`. Separate language, project, author, and university sources support the recorded claims.

Independent review supported 17 of the 18 frozen claims against seven exact source revisions. The SETL4 composite identity citation ends before the extension description. That revision remains inconclusive. The SetlX procedure finding uses the cited physical page and figure, and its review records the section-number mismatch.

Keep ISETL's documented language use and interactive implementation classification distinct while their exact historical crosswalk remains open. Keep original SETL reference editions and documented implementation deviations separate. Proposed identities and supporting claims are retained for the next qualification decision.
