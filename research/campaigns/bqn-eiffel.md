# BQN and Eiffel

The BQN technical claims apply to the pinned draft. The draft distinguishes core language material from system features and warns that future versioning may change them. The original classifier lead supports the BQN language identity at its documented scope. [Source witnesses](../../sces/artifacts/bqn-eiffel-source-witnesses.json)

The Eiffel technical claims apply to normative clauses in the June 2006 second edition of ECMA-367. Section 8.24.3 controls the attached-type requirement in the selected claim. The informative wording in Section 8.24.6 also remains in the review record. Eiffel is a distinct language referent in the pinned classifier even though E and Euphoria also use the `.e` suffix. [Lead decisions](../../sces/artifacts/bqn-eiffel-origin-review.json)
