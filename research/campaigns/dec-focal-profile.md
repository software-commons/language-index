# DEC FOCAL and FOCAL-8

The source profile separates the historical DEC FOCAL language from the FOCAL-8 service program. DEC’s 1968 manual and January 1970 manual give different upper limits for numbered indirect lines, `15.99` and `31.99`. The January 1970 scan’s printed order number is `DEC-08-AJAD-D`, while the archived filename contains `AJAB-D`. The technical claims retain each manual’s edition and provenance. [Research receipts](../../sces/artifacts/focal-dec-proof-research.jsonl) · [Independent review receipts](../../sces/artifacts/focal-dec-proof-review.jsonl) · [Source witnesses](../../sces/artifacts/focal-dec-source-witnesses.json)

The 1970 manual documents `DO` line and group targets and the three `IF` destinations. The April 1969 Advanced FOCAL specification describes the LIBRA save command as a design and marks the extension unfinished. The selected records preserve that caveat.

The original Computer History Museum page supports a link to the historical DEC language. The archive and DEC documents use the name FOCAL in their own historical contexts, including both Formula Calculator and the longer expanded form. The lead binds the language at that scope and the service program retains its separate identity. [Discovery review](../../sces/artifacts/focal-dec-original-review.json)
