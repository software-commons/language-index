# Dartmouth BASIC and later dialects

The May 1964 Dartmouth manual documents the early BASIC language. The August 2000 facsimile note and the first draft carry different dates. ECMA-55 and ECMA-116 document distinct editions and scopes. Their prefaces differ on the date of the first BASIC version. The index preserves that discrepancy and the separate dated publications.

Microsoft QuickBASIC 4.5 and FreeBASIC are recorded at their verified source dialect scopes. The QuickBASIC facts rely on the pinned transcription of Microsoft’s language reference. Independently recovered Microsoft Programmer’s Library indexes corroborate the edition and topic. The encoded database was outside the verified text comparison. FreeBASIC 1.10.1 has project documentation for the source dialect and a separate `fbc` compiler identity.

The [FreeBASIC classifier](https://github.com/github-linguist/linguist/blob/cfd7a0113aa1efab99faeff38756f0ed4d48add8/lib/linguist/languages.yml#L2422) resolves at source-dialect scope. The broader BASIC row includes GW-BASIC material, and the QuickBASIC row includes QBasic and QB64 associations. Both retain scoped evidence and unresolved whole-row dispositions.

[Lead decisions](../../sces/artifacts/basic-family-origin-review.json) and [independent source witnesses](../../sces/artifacts/basic-family-source-witnesses.json) record the 13 complete pinned specimens and the source, edition, and controller boundaries.
